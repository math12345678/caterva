enzyme_index.json.gz -- attribution

This index is derived from the ENZYME nomenclature database
(https://enzyme.expasy.org/), which distributes the IUBMB enzyme
nomenclature.

Release:    Release of 02-Sep-2026 (as printed in the header of enzyme.dat)
Creator:    the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics (ExPASy ENZYME database,
            Bridge and Axelsen), which distributes the IUBMB enzyme
            nomenclature

Source:     https://ftp.expasy.org/databases/enzyme/enzyme.dat
            https://ftp.expasy.org/databases/enzyme/enzclass.txt

Modifications (CC BY 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B)): reduced to accepted names,
alternative names, reactions, status (active, transferred with the new number,
or deleted), enzyme-class names and the UniProt entry names listed per EC
number. Comment lines are dropped. UniProt entries are kept as a count per
organism code and, in full, for HUMAN, MOUSE, RAT, YEAST, ECOLI, BOVIN, PIG,
CHICK, ARATH, BACSU, DROME, CAEEL and RABIT. scripts/build_enzyme_index.py
makes the reduction, and the same facts are stored inside the index.

Nothing here states or implies that the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics
endorses Caterva. If you use the nomenclature in scientific work, cite the
ENZYME database (https://enzyme.expasy.org/). To rebuild this index from a
newer release: make enzyme-index.
