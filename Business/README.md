# Business

Operational scaffolding for Terrium-the-company, kept separate from
Terrium-the-product (which lives in `Tellurium/`, `Tests/`, and
`Science-Agent-Pipeline/`).

Nothing in this folder is legal, tax, or financial advice, and nothing here
represents a decision that's actually been made yet -- these are checklists
and trackers to fill in once funding, incorporation, and equity questions
are actually decided, not before. Where a real number or answer doesn't
exist yet, it says `TBD`, not a guess.

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `INCORPORATION_CHECKLIST.md` | Steps to actually incorporate, once you're ready -- not started yet |
| `CAP_TABLE.md` | Who owns what -- currently all `TBD`, including the still-open backend role |
| `FUNDRAISING_TRACKER.md` | Every raise conversation in flight (Kickstart, LvlUp Ventures, SF Startup Labs) and its real status |
| `ROADMAP.md` | The phase plan from the pitch deck, kept in one place instead of only living in a .pptx |
